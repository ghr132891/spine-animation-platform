Connection mismatch execution: common-canvas registration only aligns anchors; it does not reconcile aperture width, textile scale, creases or seam paths. Inspect real connection pixels before binding. Use prepare-assembly.connections to taper a reviewed root envelope into unchanged pixels before the elbow/knee. Do not shrink the entire limb or change bone pivots to hide mismatches. For color casts alone use texture_join.mode=color; for mismatched garment seams use mode=shared-pixels with an actual co-registered donor (optionally donor_layout from this character's existing costume candidate). See skills/spine-animation/references/assembly-execution.md for the exact schema. Prefer the same garment master for adjoining parts. The executor records sources, preserves alpha and distal pixels, invalidates old acceptance, and regenerates prebind evidence atomically. No real matching donor means repair/recreate the local material in the remaining budget, not mark the geometric alignment as a visual pass. Recheck assembled, spread and motion extremes; uniform color matching is not seam repair.
